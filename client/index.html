Integrated Municipal Platform

Comprehensive Urban Flood Management Modules

Built to empower citizens and provide municipal authorities with a centralized, data-driven response console.

1. Citizen Drain Reporting

Citizens submit geo-tagged complaints with photographic evidence, precise GPS/manual coordinates, and water-level severity (Low, Medium, High).

2. AI-Based Risk Analysis

Explainable rule-based risk prototype analyzes report frequency, clustering distance, time window, water level, and generates transparent 0-100 risk scores.

3. Interactive Drainage Map

Interactive Leaflet & OpenStreetMap viewer displaying report markers, risk-level markers, and dynamic heatmaps of waterlogging density.

4. Municipal Response Tracking

Four-stage complaint lifecycle: SUBMITTED → UNDER INSPECTION → CLEANING STARTED → COMPLETED with audit timestamps and notes.

5. High-Risk In-App Alerts

Automated notifications alert nearby registered citizens when multiple waterlogging reports form a high-risk flood cluster in their locality.

6. Recurring Hotspot Detection

Proximity-based algorithms identify chronic drainage bottleneck locations, track recurrence trends, and recommend preventive civil maintenance.

The Solution Lifecycle

From Citizen Report to Sustainable Prevention

A transparent 6-step municipal pipeline preserving the original submitted project architecture.

1. REPORT
Citizen captures drain problem, GPS & water level
2. ANALYZE
Engine computes cluster score & risk factors
3. PRIORITIZE
Automated priority (Low, Med, High, Critical)
4. ASSIGN
Admin assigns specialized cleaning taskforce
5. RESOLVE
Maintenance clears obstruction with evidence
6. PREVENT
Hotspot detection schedules civil desilting
