Urban Flood Reporting & Smart Drain Monitoring System

Early Detection. Smart Analysis. Timely Action. Safer Cities.

Problem Statement & Purpose

During the intense monsoon season, blocked or overflowing storm drains can rapidly trigger localized waterlogging, impassable streets, vehicle damage, and sanitary threats. Traditional civic helplines are fragmented, lack visual evidence, and leave citizens without transparent updates. Simultaneously, municipal authorities often lack a unified, real-time spatial console to assess localized flood risks, prioritize emergency clearing, and pinpoint chronic recurring bottleneck areas.

Proposed & Implemented Solution

A full-stack citizen reporting and municipal response platform featuring an explainable AI risk-analysis prototype. Citizens capture drain obstructions with photos, GPS/manual coordinates, and water-level severity. The backend engine aggregates submissions, identifies spatial clustering, calculates normalized risk scores (0–100), visualizes danger zones on interactive Leaflet maps, and alerts nearby users. Municipal engineers verify complaints, assign specialized maintenance units, update work progress, and detect chronic hotspots.

Problem Category Up to 25 pts Drain Overflow: +25 pts | Waterlogging: +22 pts | Damage: +18 pts | Blocked: +15 pts
Spatial Cluster Density Up to 30 pts Calculated using Haversine formula (800m radius in 72h window): ≥5 reports (+30 pts), 2-4 (+20 pts), 1 (+10 pts)

HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, Vanilla JavaScript, Leaflet.js, OpenStreetMap, Chart.js, Socket.IO Client.
Backend Layer
